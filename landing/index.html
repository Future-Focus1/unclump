Why Unclump exists

Planning isn't the problem.
Starting is.

Most productivity apps were built by productivity nerds for people who already have their life together. They give you lists, calendars, dashboards, analytics — and none of it helps when you're sitting on the couch, paralysed, unable to take the first step.

The gap between knowing and doing is the execution chasm. Unclump doesn't help you organise your life. It helps you take one tiny action right now. Then another. The bar for "starting" is so low, you'll trip over it.

Built for ADHD brains. Designed by someone who gets it. No gamification clutter. No analytics dashboards. Just one question: what are you stuck on?
