Why Unstuck?

Most productivity apps were built by productivity nerds for people who already have their shit together. They give you lists, calendars, analytics, dashboards — and none of it helps when you're sitting on the couch, paralysed, unable to take the first step.

The problem isn't planning. It's starting. Unstuck doesn't help you organise your life. It helps you take one tiny action right now. Then another. Then another.

Built for ADHD brains. Designed by someone who gets it. The bar for "starting" is so low, you'll trip over it.
